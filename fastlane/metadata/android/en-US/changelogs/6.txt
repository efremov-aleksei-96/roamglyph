FLOSS foundation release: removes Google Play Services, uses Android LocationManager,
switches to an OpenFreeMap/MapLibre vector basemap, and keeps the UI in English.
Adds Room/SQLite storage for sessions, timestamped GPS points and first-seen H3 cells,
a conservative GPS teleport filter, full portable backup/restore v2 with legacy
0.4.x import support, smoother recentering, correct location-disabled status,
secure release-signing infrastructure, and F-Droid metadata.

FLOSS foundation release: removes Google Play Services, uses Android LocationManager,
switches to an OpenFreeMap/MapLibre vector basemap, and keeps the UI in English.
Adds Room/SQLite sessions, timestamped GPS points and first-seen H3 cells, a
conservative GPS teleport filter, full portable backup/restore v2 with legacy 0.4.x
import support, adaptive Fog of War with exact street-level H3 geometry, incremental
viewport rendering, smoother recentering, secure release signing, and F-Droid
metadata.

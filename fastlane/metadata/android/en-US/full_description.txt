Roamglyph turns real-world movement into a persistent exploration map.

Start exploration, walk or ride, and nearby H3 cells become permanently explored.
Tracking can continue through an Android foreground location service while the
screen is off.

Current features:
• fine-grained H3 exploration cells
• OpenStreetMap-based vector map rendered with MapLibre
• background exploration with an explicit persistent notification
• GPS accuracy filtering
• local persistent history
• portable JSON export and import
• no account, advertisements, analytics, Firebase, or Google Play Services

Roamglyph is local-first. Your exploration history is stored on your device and is
not uploaded to a Roamglyph server. Android cloud backup is disabled; use the
explicit export feature for a portable backup.

The default basemap is online and provided by OpenFreeMap. Offline vector-map
packages are planned for a future release.

Roamglyph is free and open-source software licensed under Apache-2.0.
